# Semantic and Computational Intelligence Modeling Lab (SCIM)

**Research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, knowledge representation, and computational social systems.**

The Semantic and Computational Intelligence Modeling Laboratory (SCIM Lab) investigates how semantic technologies and computational models can help us understand complex systems, connect fragmented information, and support transparent, evidence-informed decisions.

Led by [Dr. Bart Gajderowicz](/team/), the lab brings together research in computer science, industrial engineering, and computational social sciences. Our work connects foundational AI methods with applications in social services, public health, and urban systems.

## Research Areas

- **Semantic interoperability and knowledge representation.** Ontology engineering, knowledge graphs, data standards, and integrating heterogeneous information across organizations and domains.
- **Hybrid and graph-based artificial intelligence.** Combining symbolic reasoning, language models, graph-based retrieval, and agentic methods with attention to verification, provenance, and explainability.
- **Computational modeling and decision support.** Simulation, agent reasoning, and models of human and organizational decision-making for evaluating complex systems.
- **AI for societal applications.** Applying these approaches to service coordination, substance-use surveillance, impact measurement, and data about communities.
