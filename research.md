Our research brings **semantics, computational modeling, and artificial intelligence** together to build systems that can represent complex domains, reason across data sources, and help people evaluate choices and consequences.

## Semantic Intelligence and Interoperability

We study how concepts, relationships, and context can be represented explicitly, allowing data from different organizations and information systems to be interpreted and used together. The work includes ontology design, semantic alignment, knowledge graphs, provenance, and cross-domain data standards.

**Related projects:** [Core Domain Ontology](/cdo-project/), [Common Impact Data Standard](/common-approach-project/), [Ontology of Social Service Needs](/ossn-project/), and [Service Ontology](/service-ontology-project/).

## Hybrid AI and Knowledge-Based Reasoning

We combine structured knowledge and symbolic techniques with machine learning and natural language processing. Research topics include explainable information extraction, graph-based retrieval and reasoning, and methods to validate information before it is used in decision support.

**Related projects:** [SMILE](/smile-project/) and [PARLANCE](/parlance-project/).

## Computational Modeling of Social Systems

Our work develops models of people, goals, preferences, and decisions to understand how policy and service design may affect system-level and individual outcomes. We explore agent-based modeling, planning, and simulation of boundedly rational behavior.

**Related projects:** [BRAMA](/brama-project/) and [Ontology of Social Service Needs](/ossn-project/).

## Applications: Communities, Services, and Public Health

We work with interdisciplinary collaborators on problems such as service needs, social impact measurement, public health intelligence, and access to urban data. These settings test whether computational methods are interpretable, useful, and appropriate for real-world decision-making.

**Related projects:** [Community Justice Advocates](/cja-project/), [PHAC Substance Use Monitoring](/phac-project/), [Canadian Urban Data Catalogue](/cudc-project/), and [Common Impact Data Standard](/common-approach-project/).
