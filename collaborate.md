We welcome conversations with researchers, students, public-sector organizations, and industry partners whose work overlaps with our [research areas](/research/).

## Research Partnerships

Potential collaborations include semantic interoperability and standards, trustworthy AI and knowledge graphs, computational and agent-based modeling, and evaluation of decision-support systems. We are particularly interested in applications in social services, public health, and community data infrastructure.

Explore the [project portfolio](/projects/) for examples of prior work and technical approaches.

## Students and Trainees

Students interested in research projects, directed studies, applied software engineering, or graduate-level research are encouraged to share a concise description of their interests and relevant skills. Project participation depends on fit, supervision capacity, and available opportunities; no open position is implied.
